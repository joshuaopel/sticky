STICKY

You are a blob of goo. Squash into the world, cling to walls, and swing on strands you shoot yourself.

  • WASD roll · Space jump · Shift cling to any surface
  • Left mouse fires a strand of goo — hold it to winch yourself in
  • Right mouse or Q cuts every strand · R respawns you
  • Shooting spends goo and you shrink — roll over puddles to soak it back up
